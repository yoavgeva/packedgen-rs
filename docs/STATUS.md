# Implementation status

Date: 2026-08-09

PackedGen's primary backend is a PtrHash-indexed packed generation with a
lock-free mutable overlay. The repository also contains an experimental
paper-derived single-writer map, a lock-based `ConcurrentSwissMap`, and Papaya
controls. The packed generation has lock-free readers, concurrent writers,
exact striped layered publication without changed-key replay, and an atomic
`NonMaxU64` specialization with direct mutable frozen slots. It has
reproducible comparisons against HashBrown 0.17.1 and DashMap 6.2.1, but is not
yet a production replacement for SwissTable or ETS.

An additional immutable `FrozenPackedMap` prototype now demonstrates a stronger
read-only point: 49.004 requested bytes/entry and ~14.5 ns successful lookup at
one million 32-byte keys with hardware-accelerated hashing, versus HashBrown at
84.429 bytes/entry and ~27.1 ns. For mutable packed keys, packed two-choice
route metadata and a 64-KiB arena bring the adaptive layout to 54.991 requested
bytes/entry at one million keys; the read-optimized policy is 59.022 B/entry.
These are storage/layout improvements, not a claim that
the paper-derived Elastic probe is faster than SwissTable.

## Direct cache eviction update

DirectPackedCache now samples physical adaptive tables and dense frozen-base
slots directly, feeding the bounded victim reservoir without a normal logical
map scan. Batched sampling of the capped adaptive startup fallback removes the
old immortal-key pocket. Logical scans now run for expiration discovery or
only when every physical sampler is empty; the former forced scan every 256
eviction batches caused an O(n) admission cliff at million-entry scale. Exact
handle equality is still checked under the same-key mutation stripe
immediately before removal.

On the 10,000-entry/20,000-admission probe at batch 64, the retained result is
3.650 median Mops/s with 1,480 original cold survivors. Before physical
sampling the retained scanner measured 0.163 Mops/s. Indexed frozen-base
sampling separately moved the eight-thread hard-cap 95%-read mix from 2.367 to
30.947 Mops/s. At-most-0.1% proactive entry headroom raised it again, and
non-queued maintenance ownership removed the multicore mutex convoy.

The final local 1M-operation pressure comparison measured 50.968 Mops/s and a
97.689% read hit rate versus a strong Papaya uniform-replacement control at
62.353 Mops/s and 97.562%. The 10M-operation churn trace measured 34.193 versus
52.969 Mops/s, while Direct retained the higher hit rate: 80.142% versus
78.685%. The 80/20 hot-set trace widened Direct's hit-rate lead to 86.296%
versus 78.697%, while throughput still trailed 38.385 to 51.065 Mops/s. Default
batch 128 and a proactive-removal cap of 32 were both rejected; the latter did
not improve hot-set hit rate.

Opt-in async eviction now coalesces foreground wakeups and drains soft-limit
debt on a registered, restartable worker. A configurable hard-pressure
threshold makes writers help synchronously when the worker falls behind;
strict synchronous capacity remains the default. With 1% slack, repeated 10M
traces improved from 33.873 to 39.163 Mops/s for uniform pressure and 40.511 to
44.867 for the hot set. Papaya remained faster at 42.168 and 50.376 Mops/s,
while Direct retained 80.406%/86.515% read hit rates versus Papaya's
78.686%/78.684%. Requested memory is 105.110 B/entry and 2,008,244
allocations at one million mixed keys and 64-byte values; the async machinery
adds only fixed per-cache allocation state, plus the optional worker thread.

## Redis-style cache update

Direct bulk loading now builds the frozen generation from an exact-length
snapshot iterator, initializes exact capacity accounting, and safely reclaims
unpublished values on any construction error. Atomic frozen construction also
carries each atomic value through the frozen map's in-place permutation,
removing the previous second full-key staging array.

The new Redis 8.8 comparison uses mixed binary keys, 64-byte values, per-key
TTL, an 80/20 hot set, deletes, misses, updates, unique admissions, and an equal
roughly 134.3 MB live-memory target. PackedGen retained about 1.250M entries
versus Redis's 804K. Its honest 10M-operation median, including eviction debt
and final compaction, was 8.812 Mops/s with a 77.003% read hit rate; Redis over
pipeline-32 TCP measured 1.773 Mops/s and 75.365%. Tuned PackedGen RSS was
156.27–157.11 MB versus Redis at 152.85 MB. Focused new-key admission, the
narrowest operation result, measured 0.587 versus 0.536 Mops/s after
settlement. Full methodology and caveats are in `REDIS_STYLE_CACHE.md`.
